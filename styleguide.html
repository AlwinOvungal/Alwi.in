Alwi.in — Style guide & plan

Printed
with dots.

Every picture on the site — portraits, project screenshots, illustrations — goes through one halftone filter, so different sources read as one printed set of plates. Paper, one ink and a hex dot grid. Nothing else.

01 — Palette

Paper and one ink

No accent colour. Contrast comes from dot density, never from hue. Lines and borders use the rule tone; grey text uses ink-2.

03 — The dot

One grid, everywhere

  • Hex gridRows offset by half a dot, the way print screens are laid out. Never a square grid.
  • Pitch 7px · 5px7px between dots on desktop, 5px on phones. Use 5px for faces and fine detail.
  • Size = darknessDark areas get big dots that touch; mid tones shrink.
  • Light tones scatterThe lightest tones become sparse dots of one fixed size, never tiny specks. This gives the engraved look.
  • Edges dissolveFade the top or bottom edge into the paper instead of using a hard rectangle.
  • Motion is slowPlates fill in from the bottom when scrolled into view. Only the hero reacts to the pointer. Both are off when the visitor has reduced motion turned on.

04 — Image recipe

What makes a good plate

The filter does the styling, so the source image only needs strong shapes and good light. Upload the plain image; never pre-halftone it.

Do

  • Plain, light or white backgrounds (they turn into bare paper)
  • Strong light and shadow, a clear silhouette
  • Device mockups and UI shown big, with whole screens rather than tiny details
  • Black-and-white photos, or colour with good contrast
  • 1600px or wider for project images

Don't

  • Small text inside images (it turns into noise), so put copy in HTML
  • Busy, cluttered backgrounds
  • Flat, low-contrast or foggy shots
  • Colour carrying the meaning, since everything becomes one ink
  • Images that already have a dot or grain effect

05 — Lab

Try your own image

Drop in any image to preview it as a plate, tune the settings, then copy the attributes into index.html or data/projects.js. Your image stays in your browser.

07 — Adding a project

            
One entry, one image

            
Put the image in assets/work/ and add an entry to data/projects.js. The halftone is applied automatically.


{
    id: "finova-banking",
    title: "Finova Banking",
    summary: "A calmer mobile bank — clarity, trust and fewer taps.",
    tags: ["Fintech", "Mobile", "2026"],
    image: "assets/work/finova.jpg",
